The selected source builds a proof of BasicFiniteInput without hypotheses.
The main theorem applies it to the analytic implication from Dirichlet GRH.
The parameter p ranges over primes other than 2. The conclusion says there
is a prime q < p whose multiplicative order modulo p is p - 1.
